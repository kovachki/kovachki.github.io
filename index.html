Bio

I am from Sofia, Bulgaria. I obtained a B.Sc. in Mathematics from Caltech in 2016 and Ph.D. in Applied and Computational Mathematics from Caltech in 2022 under the supervision of Andrew Mark Stuart. Currently, I am a faculty member at the Courant Institute of NYU and a researcher at NVIDIA. This is my CV.

Research Interests

  • Foundations of data-driven physical modeling
  • Infinite dimensional approximation theory
  • Transport map methods for sampling
  • Data and model scaling laws in deep learning
  • Applications of machine learning to material science and fluid dynamics
  • Quantum field theories

Teaching

  • NYU, MATH-UA 353 (2026): Linear and Nonlinear Optimization
  • Caltech, ACM 270-1 (2023): Approximation Theory and Neural Networks
